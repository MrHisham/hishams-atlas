# Scientific scope and review workflow

This edition is an educational study environment. It does not provide treatment, dosing, botanical authentication or real-world identification. The garden's six stages, moisture model and daily growth are invented simulation rules, not species-specific horticultural biology. Plant pictures are original stylizations, not scientifically validated plates. Virtual harvesting never produces physical medicinal material. Foxglove is prominently marked highly toxic and its collection action is labeled observation.

Each app entry contains organization, stable URL, access date, evidence category and review status. Publication years remain source-linked rather than invented. Broad-family metadata inherited from the supplied prototype and accepted-name reconciliation have not had independent taxonomic review. A comprehensive origin/distribution atlas, microscopy, clinical bibliography, full contraindication matrices and analytical specifications are unfinished.

## Before adding or expanding a claim

1. Add the record in `src/botany.ts` and a source row here. Record the author/organization, publication year if verified, stable URL/DOI and access date.
2. Match the exact taxon, plant part, preparation, route and research population. Do not extrapolate to a garden plant or different formulation.
3. Keep traditional use, in vitro, animal, human clinical and regulatory assessment distinct. Explicitly mark categories not reviewed. A monograph assessment does not imply authorization of every product.
4. Have a qualified botanical/pharmacognosy reviewer check taxonomy, morphology, citations, uncertainties and safety. Until then retain “independent expert review pending.”
5. Review toxicity before enabling interactions. Never introduce instructions for home preparation or consumption of toxic plants.
6. Run tests and inspect the field-book record at desktop and tablet widths.

The lab chromatogram is a pair of Gaussian curves in arbitrary units, unchanged by the selected notebook context. It is labeled synthetic and makes no chemical identification. It is not a simulated validated extraction or analysis method. The compound-class curriculum, spectroscopy, validated structures and microscopy remain future work.
