## Mission reference scope

[IARC: arecoline and areca nut](https://www.iarc.who.int/faq/iarc-monographs-meeting-128-acrolein-crotonaldehyde-and-arecoline/) distinguishes areca nut Group 1 from isolated arecoline Group 2B.

[EMA: Eleutherococcus root](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/medicines/herbal/eleutherococci-radix) distinguishes traditional use from conclusive clinical evidence.

[CBD: traditional knowledge](https://www.cbd.int/abs/infokit/revised/web/factsheet-tk-en.pdf) discusses access, benefit sharing and the San–Hoodia case. The mission certifies no supplier agreement or legal compliance.

[Poison Control: poisonous plants](https://www.poison.org/articles/plant) is a general exposure-safety resource, not a taxon-specific diagnostic key.

Synthetic structure cues, dossiers, fingerprints and exposure cases were created for the app; they are not measurements, photographs, validated analytical methods or clinical records. User-provided chemical/target topics remain explicitly scoped learning objectives.
