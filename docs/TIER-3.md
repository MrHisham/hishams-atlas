The user's Tier 3 request is implemented as fifty additional educational study priorities, in the exact supplied order and Modules 11–15. There are now 150 unique taxa, fifteen modules and 165 module practice questions (ten plant questions and one teaching case per module). These priorities are not medicinal-use recommendations or an efficacy ranking.

## Research missions

The **Research missions** tab contains five interactive missions with three saved decisions each. The fifteen mission decisions are scored separately from the 165 module practice questions. All answers use the existing backup-compatible learning store.

- **The Alkaloid Research Laboratory:** classify textual structural cues, match a biological target and distinguish areca nut from isolated arecoline hazard classifications. Structural descriptions are study cues, not validated molecular drawings.
- **The Essential Oil & Resin Laboratory:** compare three synthetic GC–MS-style signal patterns, evaluate chemotype variation and question common-name authentication. These are invented signal vectors; no actual mass spectra, extraction instructions, diagnostic peaks or release specifications are represented.
- **The Phytochemical Fingerprint Challenge:** interpret a fictional marker dossier, classify a glycoside and distinguish Eleutherococcus from Panax evidence.
- **The Botanical Toxicology Unit:** choose urgent professional assessment in a fictional symptomatic exposure, compare Conium/Cicuta learning topics and reject historical use as a resolution of aristolochic-acid hazards. Name-comparison cards are not diagnostic images or identification keys.
- **The Medicinal Heritage & Conservation Review:** an additional curator-designed mission for Module 15, which had no named mission in the supplied text. Reconcile the Kew spelling of Picrorhiza, identify missing traditional-knowledge/benefit-sharing documentation and separate taxonomic acceptance from sustainable sourcing.

Module teaching cases reuse the introductory mission scenario under separate stable practice IDs. Mission decisions and module questions have separate progress; completing one does not automatically complete the other.

## Safety and sources

Every Module 14 plant is recognition-only. Additional hazardous Tier 3 entries also receive recognition-only labels; this is independent of module membership. No new physical garden beds, plant collection, toxic-material processing, extraction, preparation or administration is taught. The eight original cultivation beds remain unchanged.

Kew's record accepts **Picrorhiza kurrooa**, listing P. kurroa as an orthographic variant, and accepts **Nardostachys jatamansi**. Accepted names do not establish efficacy, conservation category or lawful trade. Exact threat categories and jurisdiction-specific trade requirements remain research tasks.

Checked overview scopes include NCCIH kratom, IARC areca nut/arecoline, CDC abrin, NCI aristolochic acids, EMA eleutherococcus and the two Kew taxonomic records. Other source links include located monograph pages or explicitly labeled taxonomic lookups. A lookup is not a verified full botanical or clinical monograph. Each profile discloses its reference scope and review status.

The supplied topic labels, provisional family/part metadata, chemistry mappings, detailed mechanisms, diagnostic microscopy, quantitative quality markers and several clinical/source appraisals still need specialist review. Conservation, traditional knowledge, product efficacy and regulatory authorization are assessed separately. No global legal classification or comprehensive clinical safety assessment is claimed.

## Compatibility

All original 100 entry objects, ten module objects and 110 questions are checked for exact equality with frozen 5.2 fixtures. Their IDs, prompts, option order, answers and sources are unchanged. Existing IndexedDB data, old saves, notes, specimens and version-1 JSON backups remain compatible. No database reset or migration is needed; use the same browser and origin to retain local data.

The machine-readable catalogue is `public/pharmacy-curriculum.json`, reproduced with `npm run export:curriculum` and regenerated by `npm run build`. See `TIER-3-SOURCES.md` for per-entry source scope and `VERIFICATION-5.3.md` for release checks.
