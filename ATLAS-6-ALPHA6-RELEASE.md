# Atlas 6 Alpha 6 — The Living World

## Implemented
- Local-clock ambient daylight and evening color/lighting changes in 3D, with existing Evening Lighting checkbox as override. No new save fields or database migration.
- Visible idle academy scholar avatar in the Quarters and Clinical Hall (existing avatar model).
- Visual fade-in when switching rooms, disabled with the in-app reduced-motion toggle or OS motion preference.
- Existing original synthesized Rain, Wind and Fireplace soundscapes remain available from Sound settings; audio requires user interaction.

## Limitations
- No fully animated playable third-person character, NPC dialogue, seamless loading corridors, or newly recorded ambience.
- Room changes still use navigation, not one contiguous geometry level.
- Alpha; verify on your Windows system, back up before using.
